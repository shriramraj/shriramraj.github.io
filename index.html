I studied data science to understand patterns. Now I use that skill to catch the ones that shouldn't be there.

I hold a BS in Data Science and work in identity and access management and security governance and compliance — the parts of security where careful analysis, clean documentation, and a real understanding of who can reach what actually decide the outcome. I've also volunteered on the safety operations team at a regional InfoSec conference.
